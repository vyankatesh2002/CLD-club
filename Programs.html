CLD Programs

Helping Children Build Confidence, Leadership & Life Skills

At CLD – Child Leadership Development Club, our programs are carefully designed to help children grow into confident, responsible and future‑ready individuals through activity‑based learning and practical experiences.

CLD Learning Journey

🌱 Foundation Stage (Months 1–3)

Students begin developing: Confidence, Communication, Basic Leadership, Discipline, Self Introduction, Stage Confidence.

📜 Foundation Certificate

🚀 Growth Stage (Months 4–6)

Students continue with: Public Speaking, Team Activities, Creative Thinking, English Speaking, Responsibility.

🥉 Growth Recognition

⭐ Excellence Stage (Months 7–9)

Students improve: Leadership, Decision Making, Problem Solving, Team Management, Presentation Skills.

🥈 Excellence Recognition

👑 Leadership Stage (Months 10–12)

Students demonstrate: Leadership Projects, Advanced Communication, Stage Performance, Mentoring Younger Students, Independent Thinking.

🥇 Leadership Graduation Certificate
🏆 Annual Leadership Recognition
