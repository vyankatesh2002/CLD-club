About CLD

Building Confident, Responsible & Future‑Ready Children

Our Story

CLD – Child Leadership Development Club was established with a simple yet powerful vision: to help every child discover their potential and become a confident, responsible and future‑ready individual.

Today's world demands much more than academic knowledge. Children need confidence, communication skills, leadership qualities, discipline, teamwork and strong character to succeed in school and in life.

CLD was created to bridge this gap through a structured, activity‑based learning program where children learn by participating, speaking, thinking, creating and leading.

At CLD, every child is encouraged to express ideas, overcome fear, respect others, take responsibility and continuously improve.

We believe that every child has unique abilities, and with the right guidance, those abilities can become lifelong strengths.

Our Mission

To develop confident, disciplined, responsible and compassionate young leaders through practical learning, communication, leadership activities and character development.

Our Vision

To become one of India's most trusted child leadership development organizations by creating an environment where every child can grow with confidence, courage and purpose.
